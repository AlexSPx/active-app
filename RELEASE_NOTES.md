# Release Notes - v1.0.0

## 🌟 Welcome to Active!
We are thrilled to announce the first release of **Active**, your ultimate workout companion designed to help you build better habits and reach your fitness goals.

## 🏋️‍♂️ Workout Management
- **Custom Workouts**: Create and customize your own workouts with a vast library of exercises, including Cardio and Strength training.
- **Live Session Tracking**: Track your sets, reps, weights, and rest times in real-time with an intuitive interface.
- **Rest Timer**: Built-in smart rest timer with customizable durations and background notifications to keep you focused.
- **History & Records**: detailed history of your past workouts and automatic tracking of your Personal Records (PRs).
- **Achievements**: Unlock badges and achievements as you progress on your fitness journey.

## � Routines & Planning
- **Smart Scheduling**: Organize your training with flexible Weekly and Daily views.
- **Active Routines**: Define and follow specific training routines tailored to your goals.
- **Streak Tracking**: Stay motivated by maintaining your workout streaks.

## 📊 Dashboard & Widgets
- **Home Dashboard**: Get a snapshot of your day with "Today's View", showing pending workouts and progress.
- **Widgets**: Personalize your experience with interactive widgets to track metrics at a glance.

## ⚙️ Customization & Settings
- **Theme Support**: Fully optimized for **Light**, **Dark**, and **System** themes.
- **Haptic Feedback**: Enhanced tactile experience for interactions and timers.
- **Personalization**: Configure your profile with height, weight, and timezone settings.

## 🔐 Account & Security
- **Seamless Authentication**: Sign up or log in easily with **Google Sign-In** or email.
- **Offline First**: Robust offline support ensures you can track your workouts even without an internet connection.
- **Privacy & Control**: Full control over your account with transparent Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Account Deletion options.
