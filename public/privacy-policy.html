Scope

This draft describes the native iOS and Android version of Active Next configured for a local training profile. The app also contains separate browser flows for accounts and online services. Those browser flows are outside this notice and need their own verified disclosures before this notice is used for a web service.

Information stored on your device

You can create a local training profile with a name and, optionally, a username. You can add body weight and height, timezone, and notification preferences. Active Next also stores the workout templates and routines you make, exercise and set details, repetitions, weights or durations, workout dates and notes, completed workout history, and app preferences such as units, theme, rest-timer settings, and progression widgets.

The native app stores this information in app-owned storage on your device. The current native launch settings use a device-local profile and disable account access, backend requests, and workout synchronization. The app does not send this profile, workout, routine, or body-measurement content to the Active Next backend in this configuration. It does not connect to Apple Health or Health Connect.

Network requests and updates

The native app can contact Expo Application Services (EAS) to check for and download compatible over-the-air app updates. The app is configured with EAS Update, and release builds can check for updates when they launch. Update requests can include the app platform, runtime version and update channel, along with technical network information such as the device IP address. Expo says that when an app uses EAS Update it may collect the device operating system and randomized tokens used to determine whether an update was downloaded. These requests support update delivery and service operation; they do not contain your training profile or workout records in the current app configuration.

Expo processes update-service information under its own privacy policy: Expo Privacy Policy. Active Next does not include app-level analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or remote push-token registration code in this native build. This statement does not mean that the app makes no network requests: EAS Update remains enabled.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Active Next can schedule local rest-timer alerts on your device. The alert content is generated by the app. The current native app does not register an Expo remote push token. Your operating system controls notification delivery and permissions.

Retention and deletion

Your native training information remains in the app's device storage until it is changed or deleted on the device, or the app data is otherwise removed. We do not have a server copy of that information from the current native local-profile workflow. We do not control device or operating-system backups.

Use Settings → Data on this device → Erase data on this device to remove all saved local profiles, workouts, routines, running sessions, preferences, saved credentials, pending payloads, cached training data, and scheduled local notifications. Confirmation is required. If cleanup fails, the app keeps local training closed and offers a retry; restarting resumes the pending cleanup. This action cannot delete data previously sent to a remote service by an earlier account-enabled version or remove existing operating-system backups. Contact the publisher using the address above for historical-account deletion guidance.

Service providers

Expo provides the EAS Update service described above. Apple or Google may process information as part of app distribution and device operating-system services, including local notification delivery. Their handling is governed by their own privacy notices. Active Next does not use advertising networks in this native launch configuration.
