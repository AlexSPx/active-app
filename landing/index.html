Your phone.

Your workouts, routines, and history stay on your device. Train offline, without creating an account.

Built for the gym with no signal.
And a little less screen time between sets.

What can I track?

Create workout templates, log sets, reps, and weights, and follow your progress through workout history, strength charts, and personal records.

Do I need an internet connection?

The native app keeps your training data on your phone, so you can plan and log workouts offline. It doesn’t require an account or cloud sync. App updates can use an internet connection.

Can I train on my own schedule?

Yes. Assign workouts to specific days or use a flexible routine and work through your sessions in the order that suits your week.

When can I get it?

Android testers can join the closed test on Google Play. The public release for iOS and Android is coming soon.
