# Active Next landing page

An introduction to the upcoming strength-training app, using the existing Cadence identity. The page's job is to explain the workout loop, show the video, and clearly communicate that release is coming soon.

## Tokens

- Plum `#2d2235`: text, buttons, video section.
- Lilac `#e3d7ee`: hero and featured app surfaces.
- Paper `#faf7fc`: reading surfaces.
- Blush `#f1dce2`: rest days and closing section.
- Muted `#76627e`: supporting text on light surfaces.
- Soft white `#faf5fc`: text on plum.
- Bundled DM Sans, regular and semibold. Fluid display size 60–112px; section titles 38–64px; body 17–20px. Left aligned, with a short centered closing invitation.

## Layout

One large, rounded lilac opening stage presents the real home screen beside a generous headline. A weekly training rhythm stretches across its base. Below it, an interactive three-part preview shows planning, logging, and progression; a contrasting plum section gives the vertical video its own quiet stage. Offline training details and concise FAQs lead into a coming-soon close.

## Review against the brief

Keep the real Cadence palette and DM Sans rather than inventing a second brand. Avoid a generic three-card feature grid: the app's own weekly schedule provides the memorable structure, and the preview follows a real training sequence. Avoid download badges without release URLs, fabricated testimonials, fitness stock photography, or unsupported privacy promises. The video and demo use illustrative workout values; label them accordingly. No automatic video playback or ambient animation.
